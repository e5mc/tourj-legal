2. Purposes of Processing

  • Trip albums — sharing photos among shared-trip members and placing photos on itinerary days and places by capture time and location
  • Member identification and authentication; isolating and syncing your data across devices
  • Providing core features: trips, places, tickets, budgets
  • Friends, trip sharing and read-only share links, and showing your nickname and profile photo (or avatar emoji) to friends and fellow travelers
  • Receiving and answering inquiries and feedback (including photos you send) through the 1:1 chat, checking reported problems and improving the Service
  • Sending push notifications such as trip invitations, a friend joining your trip, friend requests, replies to your inquiries, route questions and answers, and 1:1 talk messages
  • AI itinerary generation, booking/receipt recognition and live search — only at your request and after separate consent
  • Rate-limiting to prevent AI abuse
  • Validating in-app purchases, granting AI itinerary-generation passes, withdrawing unused refunded passes, adjusting used refunded quantities, restoring entitlements when Apple reverses a refund, preventing duplicate/fraudulent redemption, and handling purchase inquiries
  • Route Market — checking eligibility to sell and marking routes as photo-verified (confirming visits with the capture location of album photos — in a shared trip, including photos uploaded by fellow members), selling, buying and previewing routes, promoting routes through public web pages and search engines, creator profiles, follows, saves and news, route questions (posting questions and answers and notifying them), and 1:1 talk between buyers and creators (delivering, storing and notifying messages)
  • Keeping Route Market safe — content filtering, handling reports, blocking, restricting 19+ routes, handling shared-trip member objections, moderation actions such as pausing sales or suspending creators, and dispute handling
  • Comparing a creator's route purchase records with the content of the purchased routes when a trip is listed for sale, to check whether it simply resells a purchased route
  • Calculating and paying creator royalties (including applying early creators' 100% royalty period, reels mission and exclusive bonus), verifying identity, bank account and age (including through an identity verification service), and meeting tax obligations such as withholding and filing payment statements
  • Service stability, crash diagnosis and improvement; pseudonymized usage statistics
  • Compliance with legal obligations and dispute handling

3. Retention Periods

  • Trip albums: kept until you delete the photo, delete the trip (album), or delete your account, and then erased together with the photo files. In a shared trip, when the album owner (the trip owner) deletes the trip or their account, photos uploaded by members are deleted as well.
  • Account data and user content: kept until account deletion, then erased without delay (see Section 7 for scope and exceptions).
  • AI usage counters: recorded per day; all deleted upon account deletion.
  • Profile photo: kept until you change or remove it (switch back to the default avatar) or delete your account, and then the photo file is deleted.
  • Inquiry & feedback chat (including photos sent in it): kept until you delete your account, then deleted without delay. However, conversations about disputes or complaints, such as purchases and refunds, may be kept as described under "Dispute/complaint records" below.
  • Notification addresses: deleted when you sign out on that device or delete your account. In addition, when we send notifications, addresses not updated for more than 270 days and addresses the notification service reports as no longer usable are deleted.
  • Account-deletion safety lock: to prevent another device or a still-valid session from recreating data while deletion is in progress, we store only the lock state and expiry time. The lock is set to expire 48 hours after creation or retry and is automatically removed after expiry, subject to Firestore TTL processing delay.
  • In-app purchase records: while the account exists, retained to validate transactions, grant passes, and prevent duplicate redemption. On account deletion, the member identifier (UID) is removed. A UID-unlinked ledger containing the Apple transaction ID or Google Play order ID, product, quantity, environment, purchase/grant/refund-processing times, refund status, and recovered/adjusted quantities is retained to prevent the same transaction from being credited twice and to preserve payment integrity. Any mandatory statutory retention is handled separately for the required period.
  • Crash logs (Crashlytics): under the Firebase retention notice, Google retains crash stacks and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems.
  • Usage analytics (Analytics): user- and event-level data follows the retention selected in the Analytics property. The Google Analytics retention documentation states that a standard GA4 property allows 2 or 14 months, and that setting might not apply to standard aggregated reports. The Operator records the actual property setting before launch and during quarterly operations reviews. Account deletion resets the identifier and analytics data on the device; records already transmitted follow the configured retention and Google's deletion process.
  • AI inputs (Google Gemini): not used to train AI models (paid API); Google may retain them for a limited period for abuse monitoring, then deletes them (see Section 6).
  • Route Market: sale copies, promotional photos and listing records are kept while Route Market is provided, even after a sale is paused or the creator leaves, so that buyers can keep using routes they bought. Follows, saves, block lists, 19+ confirmations and creator public profiles are kept until you remove them or delete your account. An Instagram account name a creator linked to their profile is kept until it is unlinked or the creator leaves. Report, objection and moderation records are kept for 3 years after resolution; if a reporter deletes their account, their account identifier is removed from report records. A buyer 1:1 talk is kept until either person in the conversation deletes their account, and then the whole conversation (including photos) is erased; message copies attached to a report are kept with the report for 3 years after resolution. Route questions (with answers) are kept until the asker deletes them or until the asker or the route's creator deletes their account.
  • Route purchase records: kept for 5 years as records on contracts, withdrawals, payment and supply under the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce. They are also the basis for royalty calculations, so they are kept for this period even if you delete your account.
  • Payout information and settlement records: kept as necessary for royalty payments and tax filings. Tax books and supporting records are generally retained for 5 years from the day after the statutory filing deadline for the relevant tax period under Article 85-3 of the Framework Act on National Taxes, or for a longer period if required by law. They are kept for this period even if the account is deleted; payout information that was never used for a payment is destroyed without delay once no royalty remains to be paid. Temporary verification originals and transfer working copies are separated from legally required records and deleted without delay once their purpose is fulfilled. Early creator benefit records (the 100% royalty period, reels mission reviews and exclusive route records) are the basis for royalty calculations and are kept for the same period as settlement records. Identity verification results (name, date of birth and verification records) are kept for the same period as payout information; the CI and mobile phone number received through identity verification are destroyed as soon as the check is complete.
  • Data subject to statutory retention: kept separately for the legally required period.
  • Dispute/complaint records: kept 3 years after resolution.

4. Provision to Third Parties

We do not sell personal data and, in principle, do not provide it to third parties without consent, except:

  • Trip albums: photos and capture information (including location according to your setting) and your nickname in a shared trip's album are visible to that trip's members.
  • Profile photos: a profile photo you upload is shown with your nickname to your friends, people who look you up by your friend code, members of shared trips you are in, and people you invite to a trip. If you have not uploaded one, your avatar emoji is shown instead.
  • When you consent or request it yourself — e.g., sharing a trip shows your nickname/avatar and trip content to that friend; creating a read-only share link shows a sanitized trip summary (booking references, individual expenses and attachments removed) to anyone with the link.
  • Route Market: the preview of routes on sale, their lists of products bought for the trip, public questions and answers (askers are shown only by the first character of their nickname), and creators' nicknames, Instagram accounts (if linked) and public profiles, are visible to other users, and the items listed in 1-1 are published on web pages anyone can open without signing in and in search engines. Buyers receive the full sale copy. When a shared trip is listed as a route, fellow members are notified of the route title and the listing member's nickname.
  • Early creators' reels mission: when a creator invites TourJ's official Instagram account as a collaborator and we accept, the reel and the creator's Instagram account also appear on TourJ's official account, and we may introduce the reel, crediting the creator, in the app, on the web and on social media (Article 11-2 of the Creator Terms).
  • Provision required by law: when we pay royalties, we submit payment statements and withholding reports (name, resident or alien registration number, a non-resident's address and country of residence, amount paid, tax, etc.) to the National Tax Service (the competent tax office) and local governments under the Income Tax Act and the Local Tax Act.
  • Royalty transfers: to send royalties, the account holder, bank, account number and amount are passed to the recipient's bank through the bank the Operator uses (we will name it in this policy once chosen).
  • Where required by law or by lawful requests of investigative authorities.

MyRealTrip booking links: when you move from the app's shopping, accommodation or flight screens to MyRealTrip (MyRealTrip Inc.) and book a product, you provide the booking and payment details directly to MyRealTrip, and MyRealTrip's privacy policy applies. We do not receive those booking or payment details and do not provide your personal data to MyRealTrip. To show flight fares and accommodation prices in the app, our server sends MyRealTrip only search conditions such as departure/arrival airports, dates, number of travelers and accommodation area/property numbers; booking links carry only our partner number and the app screen name (nothing that identifies you).

You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Account → Delete Account. Upon deletion we erase without delay:

  • Server (Firebase): account and profile (including the profile photo file you uploaded); all trips, places and folders; globe backup (wishlist, engrave records); friend relationships (including your entry left in friends' lists); share links you created; invitations sent or received; shared trips you own (deleted for all members); your membership in other members' shared trips; the 1:1 inquiry and feedback chat (all messages and photos and the conversation summary) and this account's device notification addresses; trip albums you own (including photos uploaded by members) and photos you uploaded to other members' albums; your Route Market creator public profile, follows (yours and those of others following you), saves, block list and 19+ confirmation, route edits awaiting review, and questions you asked and questions on your routes; Route Market 1:1 talks you had as a buyer or creator (the whole conversation including the other person's messages and photos — it also disappears from the other person's list), daily talk counts and your routes' talk settings; AI usage records; the Firebase auth account (with Apple sign-in token revocation). For accounts that use Kakao sign-in, the app also asks Kakao to disconnect the sign-in when you delete the account in the app (if that request fails, or if you asked us to delete your account by email, you can disconnect TourJ yourself in the connected-services settings of your Kakao account).
  • Routes you were selling: their sale is paused so they are no longer sold, and routes under review are withdrawn.
  • Your device: trip files, ticket/booking/receipt files, wishlist, app settings and AI consent records, scheduled notifications; analytics identifiers and accumulated data are reset.

Exceptions, with reasons and handling:

  • Collaborative content of shared trips owned by other members: the trip itself remains for the remaining members; only your membership and identifier links are removed. You may leave such trips or ask their owner to delete them before deleting your account.
  • In-app purchase ledger with the member UID removed: your member identifier (UID) is removed on account deletion. The Apple transaction ID or Google Play order ID, product, quantity, environment, purchase/grant/refund-processing times, refund status and recovered/adjusted quantities remain solely to prevent transaction reuse or duplicate credit and to preserve payment integrity; they are not used to relink the ledger to your profile or deleted trip data.
  • Account-deletion safety lock: only the lock state and expiry time remain to prevent deletion races and data recreation through an existing authenticated session. It is set to expire 48 hours after creation or retry and is automatically removed after expiry, subject to Firestore TTL processing delay.
  • Processor system logs and backups: automatically purged by Google per their policies (Crashlytics is retained 90 days before removal begins; Analytics user/event-level retention is the property's 2- or 14-month setting, while standard aggregate reports are outside that setting; Gemini abuse-monitoring retention).
  • Sale copies and listing records of routes already sold: the sale copy, promotional photos and listing record (including the nickname) are kept so that buyers can keep using the route.
  • Route purchase records, royalty ledger, statements, payout information, identity verification results and early creator benefit records: kept for the periods in Section 3 under the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, the Framework Act on National Taxes, the Income Tax Act and other laws, then destroyed. Remaining royalties of a creator who left are paid under the Creator Terms.
  • Report records: kept as moderation records with the reporter's account identifier removed. Message copies of a reported 1:1 talk are kept with the report for the period in Section 3.
  • Promotional materials already published: under the Creator Terms, promotional materials we published or distributed before you left (social media posts, ads, etc.) may remain; if you ask for a legitimate reason such as privacy, we take them down from the materials we manage.
  • Dispute/complaint records: records in the 1:1 inquiry and feedback chat about disputes or complaints, such as purchases and refunds, may be kept separately for 3 years after resolution under Section 3.
  • Data under statutory retention: stored separately for the legally required period, then destroyed.

You may sign up again at any time with Sign in with Apple, Google, Kakao (new email sign-ups are not accepted); deleted data cannot be recovered.

11. Security Measures

  • Trip albums receive only display copies with metadata (EXIF/GPS) removed, never originals, and capture locations are stored only according to the visibility you choose.
  • All network traffic is encrypted with TLS (HTTPS).
  • Firestore security rules restrict access to your own data (or trips you are a member of).
  • Profile photos are uploaded only as a 512 px copy made by the app with location and capture information removed, never the original, and security rules let only you upload or delete yours.
  • Security rules let only you and the Operator read your 1:1 inquiry and feedback chat and the photos sent in it (copies the app resizes and strips of location and capture information), and sent messages cannot be edited or deleted in the app so the conversation record is not altered. Notification addresses cannot be read by the app and are used only by the server to send notifications.
  • Security rules let only the two people in a Route Market 1:1 talk and the Operator read it; messages can only be sent through a server function and cannot be edited or deleted in the app, and talk photos can be downloaded only by the two people and the Operator.
  • The AI proxy and the account-deletion API require Firebase auth tokens and App Check verification.
  • iOS file protection is applied to on-device files such as tickets and booking documents.
  • Release-build system logs keep contents private (no place names/coordinates in plain text); identifiers are masked.
  • Secret keys are kept out of source code, in build settings and server-side environment variables.
  • Sensitive data is automatically masked on-device before any AI transfer.
  • Payout information, including resident registration numbers, is encrypted as required by the Personal Information Protection Act. It is locked on your device with the Operator's public key (RSA-4096) and AES-256 before it is sent, and is stored only in that locked form on the server and database, so no one — including the Operator — can open it on the server. The key that opens it exists only on the Operator's computer, is password-protected, and is used on that computer only when reviewing identity, tax information and account ownership or paying royalties. Opened originals and transfer/tax files are kept only in a separate access-restricted folder, and logs and screens show masked values only.
  • Identity verification results are checked by the server directly with the verification provider instead of trusting values sent by the app; they can be written only by server functions and read only by those who need them (the creator and the Operator), enforced by security rules. The CI and mobile phone number are destroyed right after the identity match is checked and are not stored.
  • Route Market sales, purchase, report and block records can be written only by server functions and read only by those who need them (the seller, the buyer, the moderator), enforced by security rules.
  • If a personal data breach occurs, we notify affected users within 72 hours and report to the authorities where required by law.
