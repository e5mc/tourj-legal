2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you, keep your data isolated to your account, and sync it across your devices.
  • To provide the core features of the Service: trip planning, mapping, journaling, ticket storage, expense tracking.
  • To enable friend management and trip sharing among consenting users.
  • To provide AI features you request, including itinerary generation, chat responses, and receipt scanning.
  • To monitor crashes and performance, debug issues, and improve the Service.
  • To comply with applicable law, prevent abuse, and resolve disputes.

3. Legal Bases (EEA / UK Users)

  • Performance of a contract: account creation, syncing trips, providing requested features.
  • Consent: location access, camera/photo library access, AI-related uploads.
  • Legitimate interests: securing the Service, debugging crashes, fraud prevention.
  • Legal obligation: responding to lawful government requests.

5. International Data Transfers

The processors listed above operate global infrastructure and may process your information in the United States or other countries. When we transfer personal data outside your country of residence, we rely on the safeguards these processors maintain (including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable). All transit is encrypted via TLS/HTTPS.

6. Data Retention

  • Account and user content: kept until you delete your account, at which point we erase it without undue delay from Firebase and any local cache the app controls.
  • Crash and analytics data: retained per Firebase defaults (up to 90 days), then anonymized or deleted.
  • Records we must keep by law: retained only for the period required by the applicable law.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing relies on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the app at Settings → Profile → Delete Account. For any other request, contact us at the email below; we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 14 (or under 13 where COPPA applies). We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

  • All network traffic is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Firestore security rules enforce per-user data isolation.
  • AI proxy endpoints require a verified Firebase authentication token.
  • Secrets are never embedded in client source code; they are injected via build configuration and server-side environment variables.
  • Diagnostic logs are masked to avoid leaking personal information.

10. Notifications

The app may schedule local notifications (for example, daily reminders) only after you have granted notification permission. You can revoke this permission in iOS Settings at any time.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced via this page at least 7 days in advance, and through in-app or email notice where appropriate. The "Effective" date at the top reflects the latest revision.
